    What are the “folds” and “formulas”?
    
    
    Vanilla extract is made by soaking vanilla beans in 70–100 proof alcohol for 4–12 months. The formulas are guidelines for how much to use.
    

    
    Formulas
    
    
    The FDA Formula is a legal formula for commercial producers using Grade B beans: at least 1 gram of beans per 10ml of alcohol.
    
    
    The Co-Op Formula is based on the FDA rule but ~20% stronger — 1 oz of beans to 1 cup of alcohol — accounting for heavier Grade A beans.
    
    
    The Budget Formula is 5% more beans than FDA, for getting the most extract from Grade A beans.
    

    
    Folds (strength)
    
    
    Single Fold is regular-strength vanilla — what recipes mean by “1 tsp of vanilla extract.”
    
    
    Double and Triple Fold are double or triple strength, used to get more flavor with less liquid.
